How a word becomes a card

vocab-man is a bilingual flashcard for a word or a short phrase. Type in English and read the Bangla meaning, or type in Bangla and choose the English word that fits.

One entry is one card. Search that word again and the same card updates, instead of saving a second copy.

A card keeps the part of speech, meaning, example sentences, and the words around it. The example shows how the word is used. Synonyms and antonyms stay on that same card.

You choose the front and the back. A card can have two sides or three, and you can change that whenever you practice.

Suggestions stay on words and short phrases. A full sentence is not a card. The card shows the most relevant meaning. If that is not the sense you wanted, refresh the result and keep another.

Flip the card to practice. The English word can sit on the front and the Bangla meaning on the back, or the other way around. Looking up a word does not need an account. Log in when you want the card saved.

Questions

Can I search from Bangla to English?

Yes. Type a Bangla word, pick one of the English words offered, and the card opens for that word.

Can a word have more than one meaning?

Yes. The card shows one meaning at a time, with its part of speech. Refresh the result to see another.

What can I save?

A word or a short phrase. Meaning, example, synonyms, and antonyms stay together on that one card.

Do I need an account?

No. You can look up a word and read the card without logging in. Log in when you want the card saved so you can find it again.
