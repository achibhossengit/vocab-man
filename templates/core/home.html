Search. Save. Practice.

0:42

A card for every word you look up.

Choose any two languages. Save one meaning, with an example and the words around it.

  1. 01 Search Type a word or a short phrase. Search in one language and read the meaning in the other, or type in the other language and choose the word that fits. Suggestions stay on words and short phrases. A full sentence is not a card.
  2. 02 Save Keep the part of speech, meaning, examples, synonyms, and antonyms on one card. One entry is one card. Search that word again and the same card updates, instead of saving a second copy. The card shows the most relevant meaning.
  3. 03 Practice Flip the card to practice. You choose the front and the back. A card can have two sides or three, and you can change that whenever you practice. Either language can sit on the front and the other on the back. Looking up a word does not need an account. Log in when you want the card saved.

Questions

Can I search in either language?

Yes. Type a word in one language, pick one of the words offered in the other, and the card opens for that word.

Can a word have more than one meaning?

Yes. The card shows one meaning at a time, with its part of speech.

What can I save?

A word or a short phrase. Meaning, example, synonyms, and antonyms stay together on that one card.

Do I need an account?

No. You can look up a word and read the card without logging in. Log in when you want the card saved so you can find it again.
